About Me

Interdisciplinary educator, researcher, and industry practitioner

I am an interdisciplinary educator, researcher, and industry practitioner with a PhD in Strategic Management (University of the Cumberlands, July 2026), graduate training in strategic management and industrial engineering, and prior teaching-assistant experience at New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT).

My academic and professional interests span strategic management, leadership, organizational behavior, business analytics, operations research, project management, supply chain, risk assessment, financial analysis, systems analysis and simulation, and the application of artificial intelligence and data-driven methods to organizational decision-making.

I bring substantial industry experience in quantitative analysis, operations, financial modeling, portfolio analysis, process improvement, and executive leadership from roles at CG Realty Management LLC, Brightstone BD Ltd., SAK Structures/Criterion Group, and Accenture.

I am based in Jamaica, NY and available for in-person and online teaching appointments.
